# Leonard Melnik

**Manager, AI Solutions at EisnerAmper · Director, Cybersecurity Reach Foundation · Speaker**

I lead AI Solutions engagements at **EisnerAmper**, helping organizations move from AI strategy to secure, practical implementation. My work focuses on turning complex ideas into solutions that teams can adopt, operate, and use effectively.

I work at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, enterprise operations, and project delivery**. Whether I am leading AI-enabled workflows, improving business processes, or helping organizations evaluate emerging technology, my focus is on delivering solutions that create meaningful value in practice, not just on paper.

Outside of client work, I serve as Director of the **Cybersecurity Reach Foundation**, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to making scam awareness, digital safety resources, and original threat research accessible to the public. Our work has reached more than **5 million people** and helped more than **4,000 individuals** identify potential scams.

I am currently completing an **M.S. in Cybersecurity at Georgia Tech** and regularly speak about artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and responsible technology adoption in enterprise environments.
